1. Mark Construction & Geometry
Constructed on a strict 64x64 geometric coordinate grid with a 4-unit baseline. Stroke width is calibrated to 6.0 units (an 8% optical weight increase for small-canvas reproduction) with an 18-unit badge corner radius matching the product design system radius token.
Optical Centering Calibration (+1.0px Center Shift)

Stroke Width: 6.0px (1.5px @ 16px, 3.0px @ 32px)

Corner Radius: rx = 18px (matches product UI token rounded-xl)

Open C Gap: 16px vertical aperture, 13px horizontal offset

Inner Counter: 22 units wide (5.5px @ 16px, stays open)

Optical Center: Centroid balanced at (32.0, 32.0) with +1.0px shift

Badge Gap Rule: 15.4px (0.35x badge width) in lockup

4. Clear Space & Minimum Reproduction Sizes
Always maintain clear space around the lockup proportional to x (where x is equal to the inner counter height of the C glyph, or 22 grid units). Do not place secondary typography or background elements inside this perimeter.
x = 22px x = 22px x x
Clear Space Perimeter Rule

Minimum Mark Size: 16px x 16px

Physical stroke renders at exactly 1.5px with an open 5.5px inner counter.

Minimum Primary Lockup Width: 96px

Preserves legibility of wordmark counters across low-DPI displays.

Minimum Stacked Lockup Width: 64px

Ensures both badge glyph and wordmark remain crisp and readable.

5. Brand Integrity Guidelines
To maintain identity coherence across platforms, avoid common anti-patterns such as drop shadows, glows, gradients, or improper contrast pairings.
✓ DO
Use the official Warm Yellow badge on Deep Navy or Warm Cream canvas backgrounds for optimal contrast and brand recall.
✕ DO NOT
Never apply gradients, drop shadows, glows, outer borders, or glassmorphic blur effects to the badge or wordmark.
✓ DO
Maintain the exact 0.35x badge width gap (15.4px) between the mark and the wordmark in all horizontal lockup placements.
✕ DO NOT
Never rotate, skew, stretch, or flip the Stepped Flow glyph. The path must always direct forward from left to right.
6. Landing Header Path-Draw Reveal (1.2s)
On page load, the landing header reveals the brand mark through a smooth 1.2s SVG path draw along the conduit, followed by an arrow reveal and a subtle wordmark slide. Respects user system preference for prefers-reduced-motion.
